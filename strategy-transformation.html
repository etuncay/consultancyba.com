Transformation logic

Start with the required business outcome, not technology.

A sound sequence is often business objective → process → organisation → data → technology. This reduces the risk of solving the wrong problem with good technology.

  • Target outcome and measurement criteria
  • Current capabilities and bottlenecks
  • Target operating and architecture state
  • Value, risk, cost and feasibility prioritisation
  • Resources, dependencies and benefit ownership
