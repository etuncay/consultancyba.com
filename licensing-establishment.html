Institution design

The regulator evaluates the operating institution behind the application pack.

The revenue model, fund flows, financial projections, governance bodies, control functions, AML/KYC, information systems, outsourcing and continuity arrangements should tell one coherent story.

Critical question

Does the selected regulatory permission describe the same customer journey and operating activity that your commercial plan and systems will actually deliver?
